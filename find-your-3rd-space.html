Is this for you?
You've heard "just join something."

That advice doesn't say what to join, whether it will fit you, or what to do when you walk in the door. This call is for you if:

  • You feel lonely or disconnected and want community, but a long list of options makes it harder to choose.
  • You've been meaning to try something for months and haven't started.
  • You want someone who has done this to tell you where to begin, and give you the push to go.
What you leave with
One place to try, and how to walk in
  • One clear 3rd Space to try, matched to what's actually missing in your social life: a hobby, a group, or a kind of place. If you want help finding a specific place, just ask.
  • A plan for your first visit: what to expect, what to say, and what to do if it feels awkward.
  • One step to take this week, so the call turns into action.

If you want more help after that, you can book another call. Nothing else is required.

How it works
Three steps
  1. Book a time that works for you.
  2. We talk for 30 minutes about what you want, what you've tried, and what fits your life.
  3. You get my recommendation and your first step.
Why me
I've done this the hard way

I was isolated for 24 years. Today I'm active in more than 10 3rd Spaces. I coach from the framework I used to rebuild my own social life, grounded in Ray Oldenburg's Third Place theory, and I'll tell you what I've actually seen work.

I can't promise you'll click with the people you meet, because that depends on you and them. I can promise you'll know exactly what to try and how to show up.
