My Story

Why I built 3rd Space Consulting

This is a first-pass draft — placeholder content for you to rewrite in your own voice. Treat every paragraph below as something to edit, cut, or replace entirely.

I spent 24 years isolated. Not because of one dramatic event — because of the way isolation quietly becomes normal when nobody around you is naming it or helping you out of it.

I didn't have a blueprint. Nobody sat me down and explained how adults actually build a social life once school ends and the automatic structure disappears. I had to figure it out myself, slowly, by trial and error, showing up to rooms I had no reason to feel confident walking into.

Bowling was first. Not because I loved bowling — because it was recurring, low-pressure, and gave me a reason to be somewhere on the same night every week. I became a regular before I became good at the sport itself. Being expected somewhere turned out to matter more than being skilled at anything.

From there it compounded. Board game nights. West Coast Swing on the dance floor. A community band where I picked up a snare drum for the first time in years. Blood on the Clocktower, where I learned as much about reading people as I did about the game itself. Each space taught me something different about what I actually needed — and that not every need gets met the same way, or in the same room.

I built 3rd Space Consulting because I don't think anyone else should have to guess their way through this the way I did. I'm not a coach who studied loneliness from the outside. I lived it for 24 years, and I've spent the years since actively rebuilding a real, intentional social life — one recurring space at a time.

Today, that same framework I used on myself is what I bring to individuals looking for their own Third Space, and to businesses — bars, bowling alleys, community organizations — that want to understand why some spaces build genuine community and others don't.

Everything I teach, I'm actively living. Not theory. Not something I read about once. Every week, right now.
