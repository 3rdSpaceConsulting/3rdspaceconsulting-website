Cancellation and Refund Policy

Last updated: November 2026

This page explains how cancellations, rescheduling, no-shows and refunds work when you book a call, an audit or retainer services with 3rd Space Consulting LLC.

Find Your 3rd Space Calls

  • Rescheduling: You may reschedule a call once per booking at no charge, any time before the call starts.
  • Cancelling more than 24 hours before the call: Full refund.
  • Cancelling within 24 hours of the call, without rescheduling: 50% refund.
  • No-shows: If you have not joined within 10 minutes of the start time, the call is forfeited and no refund is given.
  • If I need to cancel: You choose a full refund or a new time.

Free Intro Calls

Intro calls are limited to one per person. If you can't make it, please cancel or reschedule through your confirmation email. If you have not joined within 10 minutes of the start time, the call counts as your one free intro call.

3rd Space Audits

  • Booking: Request an audit through the contact form. We agree on scope and dates before you pay, and payment is due before the first visit.
  • Cancelling or rescheduling more than 7 days before the first visit: Full refund.
  • Cancelling within 7 days of the first visit: 50% refund.
  • After the first visit has taken place: No refund.
  • Costs: Travel and attendance costs are covered by the host.
  • Confidentiality: I attend as a participant. The report goes only to you, and I will not share or publish anything about your business without your written permission.
  • If I need to cancel: You choose a full refund or a new date.

Retainers

This applies to Make It Stick, Build Your Community and the Business Retainer.

  • Retainers begin with a 3-month initial term, billed monthly, then continue month to month.
  • After the initial term, you may cancel with 30 days' notice.
  • Payments for a month already billed are not refunded, and unused calls do not carry over.
  • Monthly retainer calls follow the same rescheduling and no-show terms as Find Your 3rd Space calls.

Founding Rates

  • Founding prices are limited to the first 3 clients of each offer.
  • For Find Your 3rd Space and the 3rd Space Audit, the founding price applies to the first 3 paid bookings only. After that, the regular price applies.
  • For retainers, a founding monthly rate holds for as long as your subscription stays continuous. If you cancel and return later, the regular rate applies.
  • Founding clients agree to let me share their results as a testimonial or, for businesses, a case study. You approve the wording before anything is published.

How to Cancel or Reschedule

Use the links in your Calendly confirmation email, or email robertsimmons@3rdspaceconsulting.co.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated date. The policy in effect when you booked applies to that booking.
